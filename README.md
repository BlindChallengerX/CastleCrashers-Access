# Castle Crashers Access

A screen reader mod for the Steam version of Castle Crashers. It speaks through
your screen reader using Prism (NVDA, JAWS and others), or the Windows voice if
none is running, and adds sounds so blind players can play by ear.

**Download:** [the latest release](https://github.com/BlindChallengerX/CastleCrashers-Access/releases/latest)
(CastleAccess-0.7.4.zip).

## What you need

- Castle Crashers on Steam. The mod does not include the game.
- A keyboard or a controller. On the keyboard, the mod names your own keys;
  Xbox and PlayStation controllers (DualSense, DualShock 4) both work, also
  with Steam Input on.

To install, unzip anywhere and run Install.cmd: it finds the game in your Steam
libraries. Uninstall.cmd removes the mod. [MOD-README.txt](MOD-README.txt)
explains every key and sound.

## Highlights

- Menus, character select (with a short description of each character), the
  world map and its paths, the stats screen, the level results.
- The shops, the Weapon Frog and the Animal Ark: each item, weapon and animal
  read with its price, stats or what it does.
- Items, weapons and pickups lying around, read and found by sound; F8 steps
  through them.
- In levels: an enemy radar, boss names, health and attack callouts, wall hums
  and terrain sounds, and a walking guide (Shift+F3) whose clicks lead you
  around walls (you do the walking) to exits, chests, princesses and items, with hints for gates, ledges, drops
  and climbs.
- Online co-op: the lobby is read, and the mod follows your own character.
- Briefings for each level and its special parts, such as the catapults and
  the chases.

## Status and reporting problems

This is a test build. The story has been played with the mod from Home Castle
through the Cyclops' Fortress; past that point nothing has been checked yet.
Logs from further on are very welcome, even when everything went well.

Please post on the forum or open an issue with `CastleAccess\castle_access.log`
from the game folder (copy it before restarting the game; it is replaced each
time), and any `castle_17_crash` file if the game crashed. Say what you were doing.
